Need help?

If you have a question, found a problem, or need assistance with your account, please contact the Jusoor support team.

Contact Jusoor Support

Account help

Sign-in problems

Make sure you are using the email address associated with your Jusoor account. If you cannot access your account, contact support and describe the problem without sending your password.

Profile information

You can manage available profile information from your account settings. If information cannot be changed in the app, contact support for assistance.

Account or content removal

If you want help with account or content removal, contact support with enough information for us to identify the request. Never send your password or authentication codes.

Privacy questions

For questions about personal information, privacy, or data associated with your account, please contact Jusoor support.

App help

App is not working correctly

First, make sure you have the latest version of Jusoor available from the App Store or Google Play. You can also close and reopen the app and check your internet connection.

Community features

Jusoor includes community features such as discussions, local information, businesses, events, and other community content. Availability may vary by feature and location.

Incorrect information

If you find inaccurate, outdated, duplicated, or inappropriate information, please report it through the available reporting tools or contact support.

Technical reports

When contacting support about a technical problem, include the feature affected, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and the app version if available.

Safety and reporting

Jusoor is committed to maintaining a respectful and safe community. Please report content or behavior that violates applicable community rules or appears abusive, fraudulent, threatening, or otherwise inappropriate.
  1. Use the in-app reporting option when one is available.
  2. Provide a clear description of the issue and the relevant content.
  3. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment-card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
  4. For urgent personal safety concerns, contact the appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement service.

Before contacting support

Please tell us what happened, which Jusoor feature you were using, whether the problem happens repeatedly, and the device/platform you are using. Screenshots can be helpful when they do not contain sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get Jusoor?

Jusoor is distributed through its supported official app stores. Always install the app from an official store or trusted Jusoor distribution channel.

Can I report another user?

Yes. Use the available in-app reporting tools when provided. If the appropriate reporting option is unavailable, contact Jusoor support.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Use the reporting control associated with the content whenever possible. Include a short explanation so the moderation team can understand the issue.

How do I contact Jusoor?

Use the Contact Jusoor Support button on this page. Please do not send passwords or verification codes in support requests.
