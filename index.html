Account Help

Sign-in problems

Make sure you are using the email address associated with your Jusoor account. If you cannot access your account, contact support. Never send your password or authentication codes.

Profile information

Available profile information can be managed from your account settings. If you cannot update information in the app, contact support for assistance.

Account or content removal

If you need assistance with account or content removal, contact Jusoor Support and describe your request.

Privacy questions

For questions about personal information, privacy, or data associated with your account, please contact Jusoor Support.

App Help

App is not working correctly

Make sure you have the latest version of Jusoor available from the official App Store or Google Play. You can also restart the app and check your internet connection.

Community features

Jusoor provides community features including discussions, local information, businesses, events, and other community content. Feature availability may vary.

Incorrect information

If you find information that is inaccurate, outdated, duplicated, or inappropriate, please use the available reporting tools or contact support.

Technical problems

When contacting support about a technical problem, describe the feature affected, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and your app version if available.

Safety and Reporting

Jusoor is committed to maintaining a respectful and safe community. Please report content or behavior that appears abusive, fraudulent, threatening, or otherwise inappropriate.
  1. Use the in-app reporting option whenever it is available.
  2. Provide a clear description of the issue and identify the relevant content when possible.
  3. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment-card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
  4. For immediate personal safety concerns, contact the appropriate local emergency service.

Before Contacting Support

Please describe what happened, which Jusoor feature you were using, whether the problem happens repeatedly, and the device or platform you are using. Screenshots can be helpful when they do not contain sensitive information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I get Jusoor?

Install Jusoor through its supported official app stores or trusted Jusoor distribution channels.

Can I report another user?

Yes. Use the available in-app reporting tools when provided. If the appropriate reporting option is unavailable, contact Jusoor Support.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Use the reporting control associated with the content whenever possible. Include a short explanation so the issue can be reviewed.

How do I contact Jusoor?
